# Affordable Protein Rich Nigerian Meals: Beans, Fish, Eggs, and Where Addme Fits In

Beans, fish, and eggs are the three cheapest and most reliable protein foods in Nigeria today. A cup of cooked beans gives you about 15 grams of protein. One egg gives you about 6 grams. A serving of dried fish or crayfish can give you even more, along with iron and calcium. You do not need expensive meat every day to feed your family well. You just need to know how to use what is already affordable and easy to find in your local market.

This guide breaks down each protein source in simple terms, shows you how to fit them into daily meals, and explains how Addme meal mixes can make cooking these meals faster on busy days.

## Why Protein Matters in Your Everyday Meals

Protein builds and repairs the body. It keeps children growing well, helps adults stay strong at work, and keeps you full for longer after a meal. Many Nigerian meals are heavy on carbohydrates like rice, garri, and pounded yam, but light on protein. Adding a steady protein source to each meal is one of the simplest ways to eat better without changing your whole diet.

You do not need a big budget to do this. The foods that give the most protein for the least money are already common in Nigerian kitchens.

## Beans: The Most Affordable Protein in Nigeria

Beans are still the cheapest way to get a large amount of protein in one meal. A cup of cooked beans has around 15 grams of protein, along with fiber that keeps you full for hours.

### How to Get More Protein From Beans

Beans work in many forms, so you can rotate them through the week without getting bored.

1. Beans porridge with sweet potato or plantain
2. Moi moi steamed with egg and fish inside
3. Akara for breakfast with pap or bread
4. Beans and rice cooked together for a filling one pot meal

Soaking beans before cooking cuts down cooking time and gas or kerosene use, which also saves money.

## Fish: A Budget Friendly Protein With Extra Benefits

Fish is another affordable protein, especially when you buy dried fish, stockfish, or crayfish instead of fresh fillet. These forms last longer without a fridge and pack a lot of protein and flavor into a small amount.

Dried fish and crayfish also carry calcium, since many people eat the small bones along with the flesh. This makes them useful for children and older adults who need extra calcium.

A small handful of crayfish added to soup, stew, or a pot of jollof rice adds real protein without adding much cost.

## Eggs: The Complete and Cheap Protein

Eggs are one of the most complete proteins you can buy. One egg has about 6 grams of protein and also gives you vitamin B12, vitamin D, and healthy fats. Because an egg is a complete protein, it contains all the amino acids your body needs from food.

Eggs are fast to cook, need no special skill, and go with almost any meal. A boiled or fried egg on the side of noodles, rice, or bread turns a plain meal into a more balanced one in under ten minutes.

## Other Affordable Protein Foods Worth Knowing

Beans, fish, and eggs are the top three, but a few other foods are worth keeping in your kitchen.

1. Groundnuts, eaten raw, roasted, or as groundnut soup
2. Soybeans and soy products like soy milk
3. Ponmo, which is cheap and adds texture to soups
4. Titus and other small fresh fish when available

Mixing two or three of these into your weekly meals spreads out your protein sources and keeps meals interesting.

## Simple Signs You May Not Be Getting Enough Protein

You do not need a lab test to notice when your meals are low in protein. A few common signs can point to it.

1. You feel hungry again soon after eating, even a full plate of rice or garri
2. You feel tired or weak by mid afternoon
3. Hair, skin, or nails feel dry or weak over time
4. Wounds or cuts seem to heal more slowly than usual
5. Children are not growing or gaining weight as expected

None of these signs alone confirm a protein problem, since many things can cause them. But if your meals are mostly carbohydrates with little beans, fish, egg, or meat, adding more protein is a safe and simple place to start.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the cheapest source of protein in Nigeria?

Beans and eggs are usually the cheapest protein foods in Nigeria per gram of protein. A cup of cooked beans gives about 15 grams of protein, and a single egg gives about 6 grams, both at a low cost compared to meat.

### How many eggs should I eat in a day for protein?

Most healthy adults can eat one to two eggs a day as part of a balanced diet. This gives a good amount of protein along with important vitamins, without relying only on eggs for all your protein needs.

### Can beans replace meat as a protein source?

Yes, beans can replace meat for protein in many meals. Beans do not contain every nutrient found in meat, such as vitamin B12, so it helps to mix beans with eggs, fish, or dairy through the week for a fuller range of nutrients.

### Does Noodlemate contain real protein?

Yes, Addme Noodlemate contains real chicken or crayfish along with dried vegetables and seasoning in every pack, so it already includes a protein source rather than being plain noodles alone.

### Is dried fish as healthy as fresh fish?

Dried fish keeps most of the protein and minerals found in fresh fish. It loses some of its water content during drying, but it remains a strong and affordable source of protein and calcium, especially when the small bones are eaten too.
