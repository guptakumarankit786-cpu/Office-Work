Before you buy

Questions? Fair.

Straight answers. No fine print.

I don't go to the gym. Are protein bars for me?
Yes. Protein isn't just for people who lift. Expecting moms, growing kids and ageing parents need it too, and plenty of us, especially vegetarians, don't get enough of it.
How much protein is there in a bar?
12g in a Protein Bar (about 20% of your daily requirement). Mini bars have 6–8g. Protein Bars Pro have 20g.
Where does the protein come from?
Half of it comes from whey and the other half from nuts.
Who makes these bars?
We do, at Bada Barkhana, our FSSC-certified facility. See how we make it
